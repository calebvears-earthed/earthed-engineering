How we work

Five steps from site to signed off

One contractor carries the job the whole way. You get one number to call and one set of records at the end.

01
Site assessment
We walk the ground, test the soil and check access, wind region and clearances before anything is drawn.
02
Design and engineering
Structural drawings and a weld procedure sheet for every joint, to AS/NZS 1170, AS 4100 and AS/NZS 1554.1.
03
Fabrication
Cut, welded and galvanised in the workshop. Rated welds inspected and recorded before steel leaves.
04
Install and connect
Piles driven, frames erected and torqued on site. Earthed High Voltage and Earthed Energy energise it.
05
Handover and maintenance
Weld map, dimensional records and warranty handed over. Quarterly inspection and repair if you want it.
